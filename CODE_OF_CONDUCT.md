# Code of Conduct

Treat people with respect in Marsa issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Give feedback about the work, listen to differing perspectives, and respect people's privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing someone's private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to a maintainer through a contact method they have published. Include the relevant context and links where possible. Maintainers should share reports only with people who need the information to address the concern. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, issue a warning, or restrict participation according to the severity and history of the behavior.
